# Solomantalgo website packages

**Owner-maintained source of truth.** Read this file fresh for every project. Package prices and terms may change. Confirm the selected package and any custom terms in the project plan. Domain cost is separate. Hosting terms are decided per project until the owner defines a standing rule.

## Starter — UGX 200,000

**Included:** About/business information; call, email, and WhatsApp contact; gallery; products/services listing with prices; opening hours; map location link/directions; social media links; simple WhatsApp enquiry/booking request; responsive design; basic SEO.

**Not included:** Cart/add-to-order; advanced or multi-step booking/ordering; stored customer data; automated confirmations; embedded social feeds; online/provider payments; advanced integrations.

## Standard — UGX 350,000

**Included:** Everything in Starter; cart/add-to-order; quantity and selection options; testimonials/reviews; embedded Google Map; improved catalogue; structured enquiry/quote forms; enhanced WhatsApp booking/ordering flow.

**Not included:** Stored customer database; advanced booking with saved customer data; automated customer/business confirmations; provider/API payment integration; complex dashboards or business systems.

## Business — UGX 500,000

**Included:** Everything in Standard; team/staff section; one advanced booking/ordering workflow; customer data capture and storage; customer/business confirmations where appropriate; up to two social media feed embeds; merchant ID/payment-code flow.

**Not included:** Provider/API payment integration; more than two social feed embeds; complex CRM/ERP/inventory; large admin platforms; mobile applications.

## Advanced — UGX 700,000

**Included:** Everything in Business; payment-provider/API integration; advanced payment flows; more than two social media feed embeds; more advanced booking/ordering workflows; third-party integrations; greater customization.

**Not included:** ERP/inventory platforms; complex CRM; large admin platforms; mobile applications; complex reporting; substantial custom software systems.

## Updates and maintenance

All website packages include two months of free **routine content updates** after deployment. Routine updates cover prices, existing images, text, business information, opening hours, promotions, and adding or removing items in existing sections. New pages, major redesigns, new features, integrations, booking/payment systems, and significant development are quoted separately.

After the free period, optional plans are:

| Plan | Price | Cadence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Active | UGX 80,000/month | Requests throughout the month, daily when needed |
| Weekly | UGX 50,000/month | Weekly update session |
| Bi-weekly | UGX 35,000/month | Once every two weeks |
| Monthly | UGX 25,000/month | One session per month |

If illness, travel, or unavoidable circumstances affect availability, inform the client where reasonably possible. Paid maintenance time should not be lost. Project access and handover arrangements should support continuity.
