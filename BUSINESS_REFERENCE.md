# Solomantalgo Web Design — business reference

This reference is based on the owner's business master document. Use it as business context, while `PACKAGES.md` holds the current package terms.

## Identity and mission

- **Business:** Solomantalgo Web Design
- **Website:** https://solomantalgo.com
- **Location:** Kampala, Uganda
- **Primary customers:** Small and medium-sized businesses in Uganda, with ambitions to serve international clients.
- **Mission:** Help businesses operate better, reach more customers, and grow through affordable, modern, practical digital solutions.
- **Positioning:** Affordable and professional; compete on usefulness, design quality, communication, tailoring, and support rather than price alone.

Solomantalgo builds digital experiences that help businesses attract customers, explain services, generate enquiries, receive orders, and improve operations. Every business deserves a professional digital presence regardless of size or budget.

## Services

- Responsive business, restaurant, hotel, salon, pharmacy, e-commerce, portfolio, event, landing-page, and custom websites.
- E-commerce: catalogues, filtering, carts, checkout, payments, order management, accounts, dashboards, and inventory where the project scope calls for them.
- Custom systems: booking, ordering, inventory, customer management, dashboards, payments, automation, APIs, and integrations.
- Graphic design and branding: flyers, posters, cards, menus, social graphics, promotional designs, and digital brand assets.
- Ongoing maintenance, content updates, product updates, fixes, and technical support.

These are service capabilities, not promises that every package or project includes every feature.

## Customers and local context

Common clients include restaurants and food businesses, salons and spas, pharmacies, hotels, hospitality businesses, and retailers. Match features to their real needs. Ugandan projects should account for mobile-first browsing, variable connectivity, affordable operation, WhatsApp, UGX, MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money where relevant, clear location and contact details, and simple customer journeys. Do not automatically add every local feature to every site.

## Design standard

Aim for modern, professional, premium-looking work with strong typography, clear hierarchy, consistent spacing, high-quality imagery, purposeful motion, and excellent mobile usability. Each client should have a distinct visual identity tied to its brand, industry, audience, and content.

Avoid generic AI-looking layouts, repeated card grids, excessive gradients or animation, overcrowding, weak typography, and visuals that reduce usability. A visually attractive site must still achieve the client's business goal.

## Technology guidance

Preferred tools include HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, Supabase, Firebase, Neon, Vercel, Netlify, Render, Railway, Google Cloud, Git/GitHub, WhatsApp integrations, payment gateways, maps, and SEO tools. Select only what each project needs. Prefer affordable, maintainable solutions with clear recurring costs.

## Marketing and portfolio

Customer acquisition commonly uses WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Maps and Business Profile, and freelance platforms. Outreach should be professional, concise, problem-focused, and clear about the proposed value.

Examples of work or demonstrations associated with Solomantalgo include BiteRight Restaurant, Kookee, Stuwie's Salon & Spa, Joopeem Pharmacy, Drift & Dine Spot, and Home Finds. Verify project status and suitable claims before using any example in client-facing material.

## Future direction

Possible future areas include SaaS, payment infrastructure, business automation, developer APIs, review aggregation, e-commerce infrastructure, international freelance work, and digital SME products. Solomantalgo Payments is a long-term concept. Do not present future concepts as existing commercial services.

## Working principles

Understand the client's problem, audience, value, implementation cost, and maintenance cost. Recommend better approaches when justified, but keep changes to agreed scope visible. Ask for missing essential facts, make no invented client claims, and keep all client-facing work polished. The historic 25% deposit arrangement is a reference, not an automatic term for every quote.
